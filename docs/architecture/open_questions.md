decision needed before the affected functionality is implemented, not a new rule.

user also approved the policy direction for Phase 1 gates G01–G10. The affected

transition matrices, limits and retention rules that were not supplied and must not
be invented. None blocks creation of architectural directories. Recorded user

## Maintaining this register

The [roadmap decision register](../team/implementation_roadmap.md#decision-ownership-and-phase-gates)
assigns owners, reviewers, task dependencies and decision deadlines by milestone.
The [Phase 1 gates](../team/phase_01_data_backend.md#decision-gates-before-coding-the-affected-part)
identify which open questions block physical modeling or API exposure.

When a decision is agreed, retain its source references, record the decision and
date, identify affected documentation/domains, and update CONTEXT.md. A proposed
resolution is not a requirement until agreed. Do not rewrite the source documents
to conceal a conflict or omission.
